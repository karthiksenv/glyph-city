Terminal-city: An explorable three-dimensional city rendered exclusively through crisp monospace text glyphs. Architecture uses tightly spaced letters, punctuation, and numerals with clear perspective and deep silhouettes. Midnight blue and charcoal define the environment; ivory and amber illuminate windows; mint and cyan identify streets, wayfinding, and controls. Sparse coral accents mark traffic lights. Atmospheric distance, gentle rain, and changing sky light create a quiet nocturnal mood, while readable text density and restrained interface panels keep the city itself dominant.
